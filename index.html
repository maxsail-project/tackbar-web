Better conversations after sailing

Sail → Compare → Discuss → Learn

TackBar is a tool for collaborative post-sailing debriefing. It brings dinghy sailors’ sessions together to help make sense of what happened and learn from each other.

The conversation is what matters. On shore, at the club, over a beer or a soft drink: everyone is welcome.

Join the TackBar Pilot

TackBar is currently in an experimental pilot. Participation is voluntary, and interested dinghy sailors are welcome to request access.

Current supported pilot data: Vakaros exports in .csv and .csv.gz format.

Request participation by email

Contact: maxsail.project@gmail.com

Sending an email is a request to participate, not consent. Participation and privacy conditions are provided separately before participation.

Open source. Shared learning.

TackBar is open-source software. Explore the project and its code on GitHub.

Open-source software does not mean open sailing data. Personal information, GPS tracks and participant sailing data are not public project data and are not published in the public repository.
