Join the TackBar Pilot

Go sailing, then share your Vakaros activity with:

share@tackbar.eu

If this is your first track, we’ll contact you at the email address you used to send it with the pilot and privacy information so you can decide whether you want to take part.

Sending a track is not consent. TackBar may receive and process the activity while your consent is pending, but it will not be shared in TackBar Sessions until you explicitly confirm your participation.

See the step-by-step Vakaros sharing guide.

An experimental, voluntary pilot. Current pilot support: Vakaros .csv and .csv.gz activities.

Open code, private data

TackBar is open-source software. Personal information, GPS tracks and participant sailing data are not open data and are not published in the public repository.

TackBar is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Vakaros or any other device manufacturer.
