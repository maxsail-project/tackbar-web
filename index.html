Join the TackBar Pilot

Want to try TackBar with us?

Send us an email with the subject “TackBar Pilot”. If you feel like it, tell us a little about yourself — your boat, class, club or where you sail — but none of that is required.

maxsail.project@gmail.com

We’ll get back to you with the pilot and privacy information so you can decide whether you want to take part. Sending the email is only a request to join; it is not consent.

Once you’ve given your consent, getting started is simple: go sailing, then share your activity with TackBar directly from Vakaros Connect.

See the step-by-step Vakaros sharing guide.

An experimental, voluntary pilot. Current pilot support: Vakaros .csv and .csv.gz activities.

Open code, private data

TackBar is open-source software. Personal information, GPS tracks and participant sailing data are not open data and are not published in the public repository.

TackBar is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Vakaros or any other device manufacturer.
